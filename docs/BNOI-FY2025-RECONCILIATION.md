# BNOI FY2025 — reconciliation hold

## Why this record is not promoted automatically

The earlier draft import for BNOI contained these derived values:
- ROA 5.27%
- ROE 39.14%
- Profit growth 22.26%
- Equity growth 28.88%

A currently accessible secondary extraction of the filed FY2025 report lists:
- ROA 4.7%
- ROE 32.1%
- Net profit growth 22.2%
- Total equity growth 36.9%
- CAR 24.9% (while the audited report page cited in the earlier draft showed 23.91%)

These differences may reflect denominator conventions (average vs closing balance), attribution (parent shareholders vs total equity), ratio definitions, or extraction/transcription differences. They must not be silently blended.

## Decision
- Preserve the original audited PDF as the primary evidence.
- Re-read the exact statement line items and ratio definitions before selecting one value.
- Store each ratio's formula, numerator, denominator, unit, page, and whether reported or calculated.
- Quarantine disputed values until reconciled.
- Do not publish a BNOI score or rank based on the earlier draft.
